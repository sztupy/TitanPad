# Privacy Policy for TitanPad

Last Updated: January 2026

## Overview

TitanPad is a utility app allowing you to use the Unihertz Titan 2's capacitive keyboard as a trackpad

## Data Collection and Usage

TitanPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension operates entirely locally on your device.

## Shared Settings

The only data stored by TitanPad are your settings and preferences. These are all stored internally on your device and never transmitted electronically.

## Third-Party Access

The app uses Shizuku to access the internals of your system. No personal data is accessed or transmitted during this integration process.

## Updates and Changes

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing and documentation.
